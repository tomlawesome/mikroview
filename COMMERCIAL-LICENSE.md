## When you might want a commercial licence instead

The AGPL requires that if you distribute MikroView — or a modified version
of it — or make it available to others over a network, you also make the
corresponding source available to those users under the AGPL.

That's fine for most people. It becomes a problem if you want to:

- bundle MikroView into a **commercial product or appliance** you sell,
- offer it as a **hosted or managed service**, or
- ship modifications **without publishing them**.

A commercial licence removes those obligations.
