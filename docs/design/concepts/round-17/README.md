**The docket** (`the-docket.html` — the name is a proposal): one deck
card for the operator's evidence — what the engine flagged, what you
asked it to watch, and what changed by whose hand. Three tabs wearing
the metrics card's switcher idiom, each with its count in the tab:
flags carries the red 6, watchlist the purple ◉ 7 · 1 broken.

- **flags**: severity mark (✱ red, ▲ amber), pathway, plain-English
  evidence line, count, age, open ▸.
- **watchlist**: watch name, boundary, window, state chip (purple
  ◉ watching / red ○ ring broken with the reason), last event,
  mend ▸ on the broken one.
- **audit log**: when · who · what, the router's own pushes included.

The roll rail carries the card under its own name — the deck becomes
the fall · topography · metrics · stream · the docket.
