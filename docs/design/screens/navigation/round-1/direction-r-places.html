The watched things are the nav. Fall, Stream, Flags, Watch: what you look at, top-level, one keystroke apart. The hairline gap separates them from the two drawers you occasionally open. On places there is no second chrome row at all — 50px total.
Map joins the places at v0.5.0 — second slot, after The fall. Reserved in the spec, absent from the DOM until then. Nav never links to a surface that does not exist yet.

The taxonomy cost, owned: the five verbs (Live · Investigate · Detect · Expect · Admin) stop being nav — Detect and Expect survive as Flags and Watch themselves. If the verbs are how the owner thinks about the product, P or Q keeps them; R bets the *things* are how the product is actually reached.

No crumb "over" line on places — a place's name is already lit in the masthead; repeating it above the title would say Flags twice in 60px. Drawer pages (scene 3) keep the over-line, because there the masthead names the drawer, not the page.
