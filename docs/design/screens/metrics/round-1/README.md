## Direction T — the counting house

Small multiples at sparkline scale over the ratified shell. The
organising idea: **identity is the label, colour is meaning** — every
tile names its one series in ink (no legend needed, no hue cycling),
and only two chart inks exist: traffic blue and refused red
(drop · reject — the fall's grammar). The two old overlay charts
(7 action series, 16 flag types on one axis apiece) become walls of
labelled multiples; the bar-list cards stay (owner decision on #488).

Scenes, dark and light:

1. **The page** — hero stat (7.4 events/s), seven action tiles with
   now-value, quiet min/max and one annotated peak (drop's 13:52
   burst, the hour's story), cards below for the magnitude questions.
2. **Flags multiples and the quiet fold** — tiles only for the four
   types that spoke, with the min/max row as a receipt slot
   (who/when); twelve quiet types are one sentence of prose, not
   twelve flat sparklines.
3. **Hover, sharpness, table** — one linked time cursor across every
   tile (amber, the brink's colour); the device-pixel-sharp rendering
   clause (#488's done-when) written as a build requirement; the
   Table toggle as a peer view and the identity-without-colour
   guarantee.

## Verdicts

**Direction T — killed by the owner, round 1 (2026-08-23).** Verbatim:

> Did you even make any changes? I cant see a single change which
> means if you did, you made some minor rubbish tweaks. When I said
> it was uninspiring you should have dreamed big and redsigned it
> with reall elegance and flare, come up with something unique,
> impressive and wonderful to use.

What it means for round 2. T's organising choice was to carry the
ratified shell and the retained cards forward untouched and place
small multiples inside them, so the page reads as the current app
with sparklines added — which is why a change was hard to see at a
glance. The defect is that the direction was conservative by
construction, not the palette, the tile grammar or the quiet fold.

Round 2 is therefore a fresh design rather than a revision of T. The
shell (rail, groups, footer) is ratified and merged under #486 and
stays. The "cards retained" decision recorded on #488 does **not**
stay: round 2 may break it, with the reason stated.

Surviving as feature ideas, not as a direction:

- Identity is the label, colour is meaning — two validated chart inks,
  no cycled hues (validator results above).
- The quiet fold: types that never spoke get one sentence, not twelve
  flat sparklines.
- One linked time cursor across every tile; amber is a time marker,
  never a series.
- The device-pixel-sharp rendering clause and the Table peer view,
  which are #488 done-when conditions either way.
