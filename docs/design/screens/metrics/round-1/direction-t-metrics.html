a · The linked cursor. Amber (the brink's colour — a time marker, not a series), 1px, device-pixel aligned. Touch: press-drag scrubs; the tooltip pins above the finger.

Device-pixel sharp — the #488 clause

Lines land on the pixel grid: sparklines render in a canvas-sized SVG whose width is measured in device pixels (no preserveAspectRatio stretch in the build — the mockup cheats, the build must not); strokes are 2px at 1× and 1.5–2 device px at any DPR; gridlines and cursors take crispEdges and half-pixel offsets so a 1px rule is one row of pixels, not two grey ones. Bucket-per-pixel binning: one hour · 60 buckets maps one bucket to ≥ 3 device px before a line is drawn; below that the tile widens its bucket, never anti-aliases a mush.

The table is a peer, not an apology

Table (top-right of each section) swaps the multiples for one table — series × minutes, the same numbers the tooltips speak, sortable, screen-reader-first. It is the identity-without-colour guarantee and the copy-paste surface; the toggle is per-section and remembered per user.

b · Sharpness and the table. Both are build requirements, recorded here so the round can ratify them: the sparkline that is "sharp on device pixels" is #488's done-when, and the table view is the accessibility floor for every chart surface.
Reduced motion & keyboard: no animation lives in these charts at all (the only pulse on the page is the rail's receiving dot); the cursor steps by keyboard, tiles are focusable in reading order, and every SVG carries an aria-label that speaks its series, window and peak.
Small screens: the grid stacks to two columns, then one; the hero stat leads; cards follow the multiples. Nothing about a tile changes — it was already phone-sized on the desktop, which is the point of sparkline scale.
