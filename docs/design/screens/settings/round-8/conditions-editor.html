GitLab's bar with the decoration taken off. A token is one quiet pill — field and verb as dim words, the value bold in the ink the app already gives it (the address in the stream's address ink, DROP in its badge's ink, internal in the accept green, the port in the accent) — and nothing joins the tokens but a gap.
